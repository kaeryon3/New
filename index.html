  1. Consult

    A free 15-minute chat, in the studio or by video. Bring reference photos and an idea of size and placement.

  2. Deposit & drawing

    Pay the $50 deposit to lock your date. Your artist sends the finished drawing 48 hours before the session.

  3. Stencil & placement

  4. Session

    Breaks whenever you need them. Sessions over 4 hours are split across separate days.

  5. Heal & touch-up

    Message us with photos any time it looks off. Come back for a free touch-up once it has fully healed.

Before you ask

        
Questions

      

      

        

          How old do I need to be?
          
18 or over, with a government-issued photo ID that we can see at check-in. We don't tattoo minors, even with a parent present.

        

        

          Do you cover up old tattoos?
          
Yes. Dez and Ilse both take cover-ups. Send a clear photo in daylight with your request. A cover-up usually needs to be bigger and darker than the piece underneath.

        

        

          How much will it hurt?
          
Most people describe the forearm and outer thigh as a hot scratch. Ribs, feet, hands and the inner elbow are sharper. Eat a proper meal beforehand and you'll sit much better.

        

        

          Can I bring someone with me?
          
One guest is welcome. The studio is small, so larger groups wait at the café across the road.

        

        

          What if I need to reschedule?
          
Give us 48 hours' notice and your deposit moves to the new date. With less notice, or if you don't show, the deposit pays for the drawing time and you'll need a new one to rebook.

        

        

          Do walk-ins get custom work?
          
Walk-ins are for flash and small lettering under 8 cm. Custom drawings need a consult so the artist has time to draw for you.
